Why this matters

At ~70 MT / 83,000 L × 40 deliveries a year, non-reclaimable taxes at 2026 rates are roughly $760,000 CAD/year — and heading toward ~$1.27M by 2030 as the carbon charge escalates. Getting the exemption paperwork right (provincial marine cert + Ships' Stores drawback) is real money, every voyage.
AP red flags: provincial road tax charged on a marine delivery · QST on a QC marine delivery · carbon tax missing as a line item · volume not at 15°C · price differing from the PO. Stop and check.
